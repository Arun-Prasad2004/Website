# Product: Aruna Electricals & Electronics website

Surface mode: Persuade (local service business site). Platform: web, mobile-first.

## Purpose
Turn local searches ("mixie repair Thiruparankundram", "LPG stove service Madurai") into calls, WhatsApp messages,
direction requests and shop visits. Not e-commerce.

## Users
Residents of Thiruparankundram and nearby Madurai, usually on a phone, often with a broken appliance right now.
Many read Tamil first; short Tamil lines accompany key English text.

## Brand
Local, practical, trustworthy, quick. Navy #12304A, electric blue #2563EB, amber #F59E0B, mist #F3F6FA, ink #1F2937.
Bricolage Grotesque. No photographs of the shop; flat vector appliance drawings only.

## Rules
- Only claim what the owner confirmed (see src/config/services.ts and src/config/business.ts).
- No invented prices, warranties, reviews, ratings, hours, experience or brand authorisations.
- Buttons hide when their contact detail is empty.
